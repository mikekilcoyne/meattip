# MeatTip.com

The "Closing Down" letter. One tip. Issued monoquarterly.

- `index.html` is the site: a single self-contained page. Open it in a browser.
- The wall of photos loads free stock photos from Unsplash. The bottom of the wall swaps in AI images from `images/ai-1.jpg`, `images/ai-2.jpg`, ... when they exist.
- `ai-prompts.md` has the prompts for those AI images.
- `option-a.html` and `option-b.html` are earlier design directions.
