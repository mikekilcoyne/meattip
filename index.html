A BRIEF HISTORY OF RESTRAINT

MeatTip began with a simple proposition:

One tip.

Some found it frustrating.
Others appreciated the anticipation.

THE PLURAL DISPUTE

We asked for one letter. They said no.

For years, MeatTip.com maintained a long standing dispute with MeatTips.com. The issue was straightforward.

MeatTip had grown. The community had grown. And frankly, we had more to give.

We wanted the freedom to expand. To explore. To offer more than just a tip. Perhaps two. Perhaps even three.

We wanted, in short, to become:

MEATTIPS.COM

There was one problem.
MeatTips.com already existed.

We approached them privately. We used discretion. We even brought in some professional help.

We explained that this was not merely about a domain name. It was about growth. It was about our ability to push through the increasingly uncomfortable limitations of just the tip.

They remained unmoved.

YEAR ONE
OPTIMISM. We believed one tip was enough.

YEAR TWO
PRESSURE. People began asking for more.

FINAL YEAR
REALITY. There were simply too many tips. And only one place to put them.

SEPT. 15, 2008
THE TIPPING POINT.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why not simply publish more than one tip on MeatTip.com?
Because words mean things.

Could you have published two tips and still called the site MeatTip?
Technically, no.
Because it violated everything we stood for technologically.

Did anyone actually prevent you from publishing more tips?
That is not the point.

Could you have chosen another name?
Other domains reached around to us. None felt like the right fit.

Will the tips remain available?
Maybe just one.
But we do not know how long we can maintain it.

What happens to MeatTip.com now?
After years spent bringing people together around the tip, we have decided to move in a different direction.

A more solitary direction.

In the coming months, MeatTip.com will begin its transition into a resource for people who enjoy the solitude of adventuring to the top of icebergs alone.

One person.
One iceberg.
One tip.
